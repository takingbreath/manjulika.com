Privacy policy

Last revised on 12 October 2026

This policy explains how Manjulika ("we", "us") handles information when you use Manjulika, a personal assistant on WhatsApp, and this website.

Information we collect

The messages, voice notes, pictures and files you send to Manjulika, and information from services you choose to connect, such as your Google Contacts.

How we use it

Only to carry out your requests and to keep the service working. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that run Manjulika, such as hosting, messaging and AI model providers, and only as needed for your request, or when the law requires it.

Google user data

With your permission, Manjulika reads your Google Contacts (read-only) to find the people you ask her to contact. Manjulika's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Information is stored on private, access-controlled servers and kept only as long as needed. You can ask us to delete it, and you can remove Manjulika's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

We update the date above when this policy changes.
