Privacy policy

Last revised on 13 October 2026

This policy explains how Manjulika ("we", "us") handles information when you use Manjulika, a personal assistant on WhatsApp, and this website.

Information we collect

The messages, voice notes, pictures and files you send to Manjulika, and information from services you choose to connect, such as your Google Contacts.

How we use it

Only to carry out your requests and to keep the service working. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that run Manjulika, such as hosting, messaging and AI model providers, and only as needed for your request, or when the law requires it.

Google user data

With your permission, Manjulika reads your Google Contacts (read-only) to find the people you ask Manjulika to contact. Manjulika's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Information is stored on private, access-controlled servers and kept only as long as needed. You can ask us to delete it, and you can remove Manjulika's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

On this website

When you ask for an invite, you give your mobile number. To send you the 4‑digit code, the number goes to MSG91, the SMS provider we use. Your number joins the waitlist only after you enter the right code. It stays on the list, also after we invite you, until you ask us to delete it.

If you never enter the code, your number is deleted after about two days. To limit abuse, the invite desk keeps your network (IP) address for about two days. Our record of each SMS keeps only a masked number, such as 98******10, and is deleted after 30 days.

The website uses no cookies and no analytics. The pages load their font from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you open them.

Changes

We update the date above when this policy changes.
