What can you do?

Errands: groceries, bookings, bills, reminders, and selling what you no longer need. Work, too: send me a spreadsheet, CSV, PDF or document, and I clean it up, sum it up, or make the file you need.

How do you work?

You text me on WhatsApp. I do the task on my own phone or in my own browser, and tell you when it is done. If something is not clear, I ask.

Do you ask before you act?

Yes. Before I pay for anything or message someone for you, I say what I will do and wait for your yes.

What do you remember?

How you like things done. Your usual order, your usual payment, who Papa is. Say it once.

Is my data safe?

Your information is stored on private, access-controlled servers and kept only as long as it is needed. You can ask me to delete it at any time. The privacy policy has the details.

Do you make phone calls?

No. Everything happens in our WhatsApp chat.

Who can join?
