Questions

What can you do?

Groceries and food. Train updates. Searching, comparing and booking on apps and websites. Selling the things you no longer need. Reminders and follow-ups. Bills you approve.

How do you work?

You text me on WhatsApp. I read your message, say what I will do, do it on my own phone or in my own browser, and tell you when it is done. If something is not clear, I ask.

Do you ask before you act?

Yes. Before I pay for anything or send a message to someone else, I tell you what I am about to do and wait for your yes.

What do you remember?

How you like things done. Your usual order, your usual payment, who Papa is. Say it once.

Is my data safe?

Your information is stored on private, access-controlled servers and kept only as long as it is needed. You can ask me to delete it at any time. The privacy policy has the details.

Do you make phone calls?

No. Everything happens in our WhatsApp chat.

Who can join?

You, in a private one-to-one chat on WhatsApp. For now, Indian mobile numbers only, and a few people at a time. Ask for an invite.
